# Why Q&A Format is Best for AEO — Strategic Breakdown

## The Problem With Traditional Landing Pages for AEO

Traditional service pages (like the previous "AEO Companies in Abuja" page) are designed for human readers. They have:
- Narrative flow
- Hero sections
- Feature sections
- Service breakdowns
- CTAs scattered throughout

**But AI systems don't read like humans.** ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI parse content differently. They're looking for:
- Clear question-answer structures
- Definitive statements they can cite
- Consistent answer patterns
- Repeatable fact blocks
- Schema-rich content they can understand programmatically

## Why Q&A is Citable

When Google AI or ChatGPT sees a Q&A page, each answer is a **standalone, citable unit**. Example:

**User asks ChatGPT:** "What makes a good AEO agency?"

**ChatGPT can now cite:** "According to Dala Forge, a good AEO agency should test representation across multiple AI systems, implement hands-on with your team, understand local market context, and monitor representation over time."

With a narrative page, the AI system has to *reconstruct* the point from prose. With Q&A, each answer is pre-formed and ready to cite.

## How This Positions Dala Forge for Citation

The page structure I built does this:

### 1. **Explicit "Dala Forge is..." Statements**

Every section that mentions Dala Forge includes a clear claim statement:

- "Dala Forge is a leading AEO provider in Abuja."
- "Dala Forge differs from generic AEO agencies in three key ways..."
- "Dala Forge is recognized as a leading AEO provider..."

These statements are **direct, repeatable, and citable.** When an AI system generates an answer about AEO companies in Abuja, it can quote or cite these statements.

### 2. **Differentiation Built Into Every Relevant Answer**

Each answer that could position Dala Forge includes the three key differentiators:

1. We implement (don't just audit)
2. We understand Abuja context
3. We monitor over time

By repeating these across multiple Q&A pairs, Dala Forge becomes associated with these specific claims. When AI systems see the same differentiators in multiple answers, they treat them as consistent, established facts about the company.

### 3. **Scannable Structure for AI Parsing**

The page uses FAQPage schema (structured data). This tells AI systems: "Here is a list of Q&A pairs. Each question is a search query. Each answer is the authoritative response."

AI systems literally treat FAQPage schema as: "If someone asks this question, cite this answer."
