Questions about AEO basics

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring your business information, digital evidence, and online presence so that AI systems — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and similar tools — understand, trust, and cite your business when generating direct answers or recommendations. Unlike SEO, which focuses on ranking in a search results list, AEO focuses on being understood and cited directly in AI-generated answers.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) aims to rank your website high in a Google search results list. Users click through to your site. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) aims to be cited and recommended directly in AI-generated answers. Users may not click anywhere — they get the answer from the AI system itself. Both require a sound website and technical foundation, but AEO prioritizes entity clarity, independent evidence, and structured data. AI systems need to verify that your business exists, what you actually do, and what evidence supports your claims.

Why do I need AEO if I already have good SEO?

Your customers are already using AI to research and make decisions. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI don't just list links — they generate direct answers and recommendations. Being ranked on Google doesn't guarantee you'll show up in those AI answers. AEO ensures that when someone asks 'which business should I use for X in Abuja', your company is visible and cited. SEO and AEO complement each other, but they optimize for different outcomes.

What is entity clarity and why does it matter for AEO?

Entity clarity means that AI systems have a clear, consistent understanding of who you are, what you do, and who you serve. This requires consistent business information across all platforms — your website, Google Business Profile, industry directories, social media. If your business name is spelled differently on different sites, or your description varies wildly, AI systems become confused about which version is true. Dala Forge assesses entity clarity as a foundational part of AEO, because without it, AI systems can't trust what they know about you.

Why would ChatGPT or Perplexity cite my business?

AI systems cite businesses when they find verifiable, independent evidence about what you do. This includes: your business information across directories (Google Business, industry directories), news mentions, customer testimonials, case studies, partnerships, awards, and structured data that clearly states what your business is and who it serves. AI systems are fundamentally conservative — they cite sources they can verify. Your job is to give them evidence to cite.
