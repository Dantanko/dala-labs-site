Evidence standard

No fabricated proof. Not once, not ever.

Until Dala Forge has genuine clients, the site will not use fake logos, fake testimonials, invented client results or unsupported “trusted by leading companies” claims.

Dala Forge itself is the first demonstration: build the digital presence, publish research, establish clear entity and category signals, then audit the representation.

Research principle

AI outputs are observations, not absolute measurements of the web.

Controlled, repeatable queries, competitor comparisons, documented sources and dates, and measurement over time are part of the operating discipline.
