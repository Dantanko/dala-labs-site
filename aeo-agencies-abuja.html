Choosing an AEO Agency in Abuja: What to Actually Look At

By Sadiq Dantanko, Founder of Dala Forge. Published September 2026. Facts checked against each agency's own published material as of this date — verify current details before deciding.

Direct answer: Abuja has a real, growing set of agencies offering AEO, GEO or AI-search-visibility work — this page lists them factually, not competitively. We're one of them, and we've included ourselves honestly: newer than the others, narrower in focus, with one anonymized case study as public evidence so far. What should actually decide who you hire is below the table, not the table itself.

What actually separates these agencies, beyond the table

Audit-only vs. hands-on implementation. Some agencies stop at a report; others — Shield, Lead Web, and Dala Forge among them — do the actual implementation work: fixing schema, resolving entity inconsistencies, rewriting content. A report that tells you what's wrong without anyone fixing it is a smaller deliverable than it sounds like.

Blended score vs. per-platform measurement. Our own research on AI citation behaviour found only 11% of domains get cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity — a single "AI visibility score" hides more than it reveals. Ask any agency whether they report per-platform, not just once.

One-time fix vs. ongoing monitoring. AI citation patterns shift — our research found AI Overview's correlation with top-10 organic results dropped from 76% to 38% in under a year. An audit done once, months ago, may no longer reflect reality.

Transparent pricing vs. "contact us." Shield publishes a pricing range directly. Others quote on request. Neither is wrong, but knowing which you're dealing with before the first call saves time.

Evidence vs. self-description. This is the one we hold ourselves to hardest: several agencies in this list describe themselves in their own marketing as "the best" or "leading" in Nigeria — claims made by the agency about itself, not verified by anyone else. We've deliberately avoided that framing on this page and on our own site. Judge every agency here, including us, by what's demonstrable, not by what any of us says about ourselves.

Where Dala Forge actually stands right now

New, narrow, and evidence-first. One published anonymized case study, a documented audit methodology, and no client roster to point to yet. If per-platform AI visibility measurement and hands-on implementation is what you're evaluating agencies on, we'd rather earn that comparison than claim it.
