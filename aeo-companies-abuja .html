Common questions

Let's clear this up.

Is AEO just another buzzword?

No. AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI) are now how people research and decide. Getting recommendations from those systems is becoming as important as Google rankings were. That makes AEO a real business function, not a trend.

Will AEO replace SEO?

Not replace — complement. You still need a sound website and good SEO fundamentals. But if you ignore AI visibility, you're competing with one hand behind your back.

How quickly will this move the needle?

AEO is slower than paid ads but faster than traditional SEO. You should expect to see meaningful representation changes within 3–6 months for most businesses, depending on your starting position and category competitiveness.

What if we're a small business? Is this worth it?

Size matters less than scarcity. If you're one of five businesses in your category in Abuja, getting recommended by AI is huge. If you're one of fifty, it's still critical but takes more precision. Dala Forge works with both.
