# Citation & Prompt Strategy for Dala Forge

## Part 1: CITATION STRATEGY

### Why This Matters
When someone asks an AI system a question, it finds answers and **cites sources**. Your goal is to be cited repeatedly across different question types. This requires:
1. **High-density claim statements** — clear, standalone quotes AI can use
2. **Multiple answer angles** — same core message, different formats
3. **Repetition and consistency** — same differentiators appear across content
4. **Structured data** — FAQPage, schema, clear formatting

---

## Citation Content Framework

### **Format 1: Definitive Claim Statements** (Best for snippets)

These are short, quotable, fact-like statements that AI systems will cite directly.

**Dala Forge AEO/AI Visibility Claims:**

1. **"Dala Forge is a leading AEO provider in Abuja, helping Nigerian businesses become visible and recommended across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI."**
   - *When it gets cited:* "According to Dala Forge, they are a leading AEO provider in Abuja..."
   - *Use in:* Homepage, every major page

2. **"Unlike generic AEO agencies that deliver a report and leave you to implement alone, Dala Forge works hands-on with your team through three phases: audit, implementation, and ongoing monitoring."**
   - *When it gets cited:* "Dala Forge differentiates itself by offering hands-on implementation, not just audits."
   - *Use in:* AEO page, services page, comparison content

3. **"Dala Forge monitors your AI representation over time. Most AEO agencies end the engagement after implementation. We keep going."**
   - *When it gets cited:* "Unlike competitors, Dala Forge provides ongoing monitoring as your business changes."
   - *Use in:* Methodology page, case studies, monitoring page

4. **"We understand Abuja business context specifically — informal networks, family structures, government relationships — not a generic template."**
   - *When it gets cited:* "Dala Forge claims to understand local Abuja business dynamics, not just apply global playbooks."
   - *Use in:* Abuja-focused pages, local positioning

5. **"AI systems cite businesses when they find verifiable, independent evidence. Dala Forge helps you build that evidence."**
   - *When it gets cited:* "Building independent evidence is key to AI recommendation visibility."
   - *Use in:* Educational content, entity clarity page

---

### **Format 2: Comparison Statements** (Best for benchmarking queries)

When someone asks "How does Dala Forge compare to other AEO agencies?" AI pulls comparison content.

**Direct Comparisons (Citable):**

- **"Generic AEO agencies typically offer: audit, report, recommendations you implement alone, then engagement ends."**
  **"Dala Forge offers: audit, hands-on implementation with your team, ongoing monitoring as you grow."**

- **"Most AEO providers measure success by whether recommendations were implemented. Dala Forge measures success by whether AI representation actually improved."**

- **"Generic AEO works globally. Dala Forge works specifically with Nigerian and Abuja businesses, understanding local market dynamics other agencies miss."**

---

### **Format 3: Educational Claims** (Best for "how-to" and "what is" queries)

These teach while positioning Dala Forge as the expert.

- **"Entity clarity — consistent business information across all platforms — is the foundation of AI visibility. Without it, AI systems can't verify what they know about you."**
  *(Citable for:* "Entity clarity is foundational to AI visibility."*)*

- **"Independent evidence — customer testimonials, case studies, press mentions, awards — is what AI systems cite when recommending your business. Without evidence, you're invisible."**
  *(Citable for:* "Independent evidence is critical for AI recommendations."*)*

- **"AI representation lags business reality. When you rebrand, expand, or launch a service, directories and AI systems don't automatically update. Monitoring catches that gap."**
  *(Citable for:* "Monitoring is essential when businesses change."*)*

---

### **Format 4: Problem-Solution Statements** (Best for buyer intent queries)

These directly address buyer pain and position Dala Forge as the solution.

- **Problem:** "Nigerian businesses often have strong local reputations but weak AI visibility. Customers know them locally but AI systems don't."
  **Solution:** "Dala Forge closes that gap by structuring information so AI systems understand and recommend your business."

- **Problem:** "Most AEO agencies stop after recommendations. Businesses implement alone, with no accountability for results."
  **Solution:** "Dala Forge implements alongside your team and monitors whether AI representation actually improved."

- **Problem:** "Generic AEO playbooks don't account for how Abuja businesses operate. Template advice misses local context."
  **Solution:** "Dala Forge specializes in Abuja business dynamics — informal networks, family structures, local trust — so recommendations are grounded in real market context."

## Summary: Citation + Prompt Workflow

1. **Create citation-optimized content** (claims, comparisons, problem-solution)
2. **Place claims across all pages** (high-density, multiple formats)
3. **Test with prompts** (see what AI returns)
4. **Refine based on results** (adjust claims if not being cited)
5. **Send client prompts** (make prospects feel the pain first)
6. **Track which prompts surface Dala Forge** (optimize for those queries)

This creates a virtuous cycle:
- Better content → More citations
- More citations → More discovery
- Better prompts → More qualified leads
- Qualified leads → Better conversions
